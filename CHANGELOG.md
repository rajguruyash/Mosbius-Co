# Changelog

Changes to Mosbius & Co.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Eight locations.** Ted's apartment, the bedrooms, MacLaren's (entered from the street), GNB, the rooftop, Barney's apartment, Columbia and the laser tag arena, all original pixel art. Switch with the tabs above the floor or keys 1 to 8.
- **A team per location.** Each location has its own boss and roster. An agent joins the location that is open when you add it, and keeps running wherever you look.
- **Doors and transitions.** Agents walk between locations through the doors; changing location opens like a pair of doors, and the pub is entered from the street.
- **The cast.** Ted, Marshall, Lily, Robin, Barney, Tracy, Penny, Luke, Ranjit, Carl, Wendy, the GNB staff and more.
- **First launch.** A pixel photo album, Ted's welcome and a plain-language workspace picker.
- **Dark theme by default**, a Ted app icon and a Mac DMG build.

### Removed

- The upstream office theme, its maps and the paid tile pack they used.
